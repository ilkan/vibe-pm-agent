# Request Bedrock Model Access

## Steps to Request Model Access:

1. **Open AWS Console**: Go to https://console.aws.amazon.com
2. **Navigate to Bedrock**: Search for "Bedrock" in the services
3. **Go to Model Access**: In the left sidebar, click "Model access"
4. **Request Access**: Click "Request model access" or "Manage model access"
5. **Select Models**: Choose these models:
   - ✅ **Amazon Nova Pro** (amazon.nova-pro-v1:0)
   - ✅ **Amazon Nova Lite** (amazon.nova-lite-v1:0) 
   - ✅ **Claude 3.5 Sonnet** (anthropic.claude-3-5-sonnet-20241022-v2:0)
   - ✅ **Claude 3 Haiku** (anthropic.claude-3-haiku-20240307-v1:0)

6. **Submit Request**: Fill out the use case form and submit

## Use Case Information:
- **Use Case**: Product Management AI Assistant
- **Description**: Building an AI-powered PM agent for business analysis, document generation, and interview preparation
- **Industry**: Technology/Software Development
- **Expected Usage**: Development and testing of PM tools

## Wait Time:
- **Amazon Nova**: Usually instant approval
- **Claude Models**: May take a few minutes to hours

## Check Status:
After requesting, you can check the status in the "Model access" page. Look for "Access granted" status.
