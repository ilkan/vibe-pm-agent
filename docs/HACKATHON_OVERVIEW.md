The Vibe PM Agent is the missing "PM Mode" for Kiro IDE - the first strategic business intelligence layer specifically designed for developer tools. It transforms raw technical ideas into executive-ready business cases through AI-powered analysis with professional consulting-grade quality.

- **Executive Communications**: Board-ready presentations using Pyramid Principle
- **Strategic Validation**: Market timing, competitive analysis, ROI projections
