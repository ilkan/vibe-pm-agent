## 🎯 Market Opportunity Analysis

### TAM/SAM/SOM Methodology
**Total Addressable Market (TAM)**
- Complete market universe for the solution
- Industry-wide data from Gartner, Forrester, IDC
- Geographic and demographic segmentation

**Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**
- Portion of TAM targeted by your business model
- Competitive landscape and positioning analysis
- Technology adoption curves and market maturity

**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**
- Realistic market share achievable
- Resource constraints and competitive advantages
- Go-to-market strategy and execution capabilities

## 📊 Confidence Scoring Methodology

### Scoring Algorithm
```
Confidence Score = (Source Quality × 0.4) + (Evidence Quantity × 0.3) + (Methodology Rigor × 0.3)
```

**Source Quality (40% weight)**
- A-tier sources (McKinsey, BCG, Gartner): 90-100 points
- B-tier sources (Industry reports, surveys): 70-89 points  
- C-tier sources (Company data, estimates): 50-69 points

**Evidence Quantity (30% weight)**
- 20+ citations: 90-100 points
- 10-19 citations: 70-89 points
- 5-9 citations: 50-69 points

**Methodology Rigor (30% weight)**
- Peer-reviewed research: 90-100 points
- Professional analysis: 70-89 points
- Industry estimates: 50-69 points

### Confidence Interpretation
- **85-100%**: High confidence - Proceed with investment
- **70-84%**: Medium confidence - Additional validation recommended
- **50-69%**: Low confidence - Significant research needed
- **<50%**: Insufficient evidence - Do not proceed
